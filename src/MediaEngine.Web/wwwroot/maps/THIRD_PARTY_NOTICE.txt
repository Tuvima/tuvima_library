The bundled world boundary dataset is Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0 Countries data.
Natural Earth data is in the public domain. See https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.

MapLibre GL JS is distributed under the BSD 3-Clause License. Its full license is
stored at ../vendor/maplibre/LICENSE.txt.

When network access is available, Tuvima Atlas uses OpenFreeMap's hosted MapLibre
style with map data from OpenStreetMap contributors. The bundled Natural Earth
world layer remains the offline fallback.
